      A NOTE ABOUT THE PAST

      The archive has gaps.

      The oldest surviving capture isn't a website's launch date, and a missing year doesn't mean it was offline. Wayback may redirect to another year or load newer assets, and old replays can lose images, scripts and styles. A screenshot made today is not proof of exactly what visitors saw then.
